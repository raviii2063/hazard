Simulator Training Manual

Hazard Perception Gameplay & Touchpad Controls

1

360° Looking (Touchpad & Mouse)

Look around the 3D warehouse environment smoothly using multiple input methods:

  • Touchpad / Touch Screen: Click/touch and Drag anywhere on the 3D view to rotate camera 360°.
  • Mouse Pointer Lock: Click the 3D view to lock mouse for high-sensitivity aiming.
  • W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys to walk around.
  • Use the on-screen Virtual D-Pad to walk on laptop touchpads or mobile displays.
2

Easy Hazard Spotting

Search the warehouse for dangerous situations:

  • Look for glowing yellow pulsing beacon rings on the floor.
  • Aim your center crosshair directly at a potential hazard object.
  • Press E, Left Click, or tap the on-screen ⚠️ SPOT HAZARD button.
  • Select the correct hazard category to earn 20 points!
3

Scoring & Continuous Flow

Maximise your training score:

  • Max Score: 100 Points per scenario.
  • Continuous Flow: After identifying a hazard, you immediately resume walking without re-clicking!
  • Educational Reveal: Any missed hazards are fully revealed at the end with safe solutions.
4

Educational Safety Quiz

Consolidate your safety learning:

  • Complete 5 practical, easy-to-understand safety quiz questions.
  • Review instant feedback and detailed safety guidance explanations.
  • Your overall score (out of 100) is saved to SQLite.
